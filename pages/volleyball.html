My Journey

Started late, improving fast.

I started playing beach volleyball about 4.5 years ago, which means I have had a lot of catching up to do compared with people who grew up in the sport. That is also what makes the chase exciting.

I’m currently ranked Top 20 in Norway and around Top 800 in the world. This season I finally broke through with my first Norwegian Tour win, and then followed it up with another one.

On the Norwegian Tour I have now collected 2 gold medals, 3 silver medals, and 8 bronze medals, while also placing 5th at the Norwegian Championship. The results feel more stable, the level keeps rising, and I have more belief than ever in what I’m building.

I still feel like I am early in the process, and that is what makes the next season exciting.
